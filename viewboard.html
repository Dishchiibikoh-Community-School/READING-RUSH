📋 HOW TO PLAY — ViewBoard Mode

🏁 THE RACE

  • 1️⃣ Split class into 2–6 teams
  • 2️⃣ A literacy question appears on screen
  • 3️⃣ Teams buzz in by tapping their team button
  • 4️⃣ The buzzed team picks an answer (A/B/C/D)
  • 5️⃣ Correct = car moves forward! Wrong = engine stall!

⚡ SCORING & STREAKS

  • ✅ Correct answer = 100 pts
  • ⚡ Fast answer (<5s) = +50 bonus
  • 🔥 3 in a row = TURBO ×1.5
  • 🔥🔥 5 in a row = NITRO ×2.0!
  • ❌ Wrong answer = streak resets, car stalls

🎁 POWER-UPS

  • 🚀 Turbo Boost — 2× points next correct
  • ❄️ Freeze — freeze the leader for 1 question
  • 🛡️ Shield — block one wrong penalty
  • 💣 Eliminator — remove 2 wrong answers
  • 🌀 Draft — steal 50 pts from nearest team

🏆 CHAMPIONSHIP ROUNDS

  • ⭐ Round 1 — Pit Stop (warm-up)
  • ⭐⭐ Round 2 — Street Race (harder)
  • ⭐⭐⭐ Round 3 — Grand Prix (advanced)
  • 🏁 PHOTO FINISH — top 2 teams head-to-head!
  • 🏆 Final podium with winner celebration!

💡 Teacher Tip: Each question has a timer. Teams earn power-ups every 4 correct answers. Use ⏸️ Pause to stop the race anytime. Hit 🏠 End to finish early.
